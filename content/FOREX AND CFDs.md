What is retail Forex trading?

Retail Forex trading means **ordinary individuals** — not banks or large institutions — buying and selling currencies through an online broker to make a profit from price movements.

The word **"retail"** simply means individual/personal level — just like retail shopping means buying for yourself rather than in bulk for a business.

### Maturity of Futures Contracts

Maturity (also called the expiration date) is the predetermined date in the future on which a futures contract must be settled — meaning the transaction must be completed, either by:

- Delivering the actual asset, or
    
- Closing out the position with a cash settlement
