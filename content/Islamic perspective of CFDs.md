#### Islamic Perspective on CFDs

CFDs are considered **highly problematic** in Islamic finance — arguably even more so than regular Forex:

---

##### ❌ Problem 1 — No Real Ownership

- Islamic trade requires **actual ownership** of what you buy and sell
- In CFDs you never own anything — just a price contract
- Directly violates the principle of **Qabdh (possession)**

---

##### ❌ Problem 2 — Pure Speculation

- CFDs serve **no real economic purpose**
- No goods change hands, no services rendered
- Pure betting on price direction = **Maysir (gambling)**

---

##### ❌ Problem 3 — Short Selling

- CFDs make it very easy to short sell
- Selling something you never owned
- Directly violates the hadith:

> **"Do not sell what you do not have"** _(Sunan Abu Dawood)_

---

##### ❌ Problem 4 — Riba Through Overnight Fees

- Holding CFD positions overnight attracts swap fees
- Pure interest charged on leveraged positions
- **Riba** in its most direct form

---

##### ❌ Problem 5 — Gharar

- The underlying asset is never delivered or possessed
- The entire contract is based on **uncertain future price movement**
- Maximum Gharar — uncertainty built into the very structure of the product
