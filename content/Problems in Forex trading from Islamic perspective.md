[[FOREX AND CFDs]]
### Problem 1 — Riba (Interest)

#### What happens in Forex:

- Every position held **overnight** attracts a swap fee
- This swap fee is essentially **interest charged for keeping a leveraged position open**
- The longer you hold — the more interest accumulates
- Even Islamic accounts often charge **administrative holding fees** which scholars say is the same thing with a different name

#### Why it is a problem in Islam:

- Riba is one of the **most severely prohibited** things in Islam
- Allah does not just prohibit it — He declares **war** on those who practice it:

> **"O you who believe! Fear Allah and give up what remains of your demand for Riba, if you are indeed believers. If you do not, take notice of war from Allah and His Messenger"** _(Surah Al-Baqarah 2:278-279)_


#### The severity:

- The Prophet ﷺ said:

> **"Riba has seventy three doors, the least of which is equivalent to a man committing adultery with his own mother"** _(Sunan Ibn Majah)_

### Problem 2 — Gharar (Excessive Uncertainty)

#### What happens in Forex:

- No one — not banks, not analysts, not governments — can predict with certainty where currency prices will move
- Traders open positions purely based on **guesswork, charts, and speculation**
- The outcome of every trade is fundamentally **unknown and uncertain**
- High leverage multiplies this uncertainty to extreme levels

#### Why it is a problem in Islam:

- Islamic contracts require **clarity and certainty** in all terms
- The subject matter, price, and delivery must all be **clearly defined and known**
- The Prophet ﷺ said:

> **"The Prophet ﷺ forbade transactions involving Gharar"** _(Sahih Muslim)_

### Problem 3 — Maysir (Gambling)

#### What happens in Forex:

- Traders put money at risk hoping to **gain more money** purely based on chance and prediction
- For every person who wins — someone else **loses the exact same amount**
- High leverage means you can **lose everything in minutes**
- Most retail traders (studies show **70–80%**) lose money consistently
- The market does not care about your analysis — prices move based on **millions of factors** beyond any individual's control

#### Why it is a problem in Islam:

- Gambling is explicitly prohibited in the Quran:

> **"O you who believe! Intoxicants, gambling, stone altars and divining arrows are an abomination of Satan's handiwork — so avoid them so that you may be successful"** _(Surah Al-Maidah 5:90)_

#### How Forex resembles gambling:

| Gambling                   | Forex Trading            |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Put money at risk          | Deposit margin           |
| Outcome is uncertain       | Price movement unknown   |
| Winner takes loser's money | Zero sum market          |
| Addictive behavior         | Highly addictive trading |
### Problem 4 — Bay' al-Ma'dum (Selling What You Do Not Own)

#### What happens in Forex:

- When you **short sell** a currency pair — you are selling a currency you do not actually possess
- You borrow it from the broker, sell it, hope it falls in price, then buy it back cheaper
- At no point do you **actually own** the currency you are selling
- Even in long trades — with leverage — you only own a **tiny fraction** of what you are trading

#### Why it is a problem in Islam:

- The Prophet ﷺ directly prohibited this:

> **"Do not sell what you do not have"** _(Sunan Abu Dawood — Hakim ibn Hizam رضي الله عنه)_
### Problem 5 — No Real Qabdh (Physical Possession)

#### What happens in Forex:

- In genuine Islamic currency exchange — money must change hands **immediately**
- In Forex — no currency is ever actually delivered or transferred
- You are trading a **contract based on price** — not the actual currency itself
- Positions are settled in **cash profit or loss** — not by actual currency delivery

#### Why it is a problem in Islam:

- The Prophet ﷺ established the rule of hand-to-hand exchange for currencies:

> **"Gold for gold, silver for silver — hand to hand. Whoever pays more or takes more has engaged in Riba"** _(Sahih Muslim)_

- Currency exchange without actual possession violates the **Qabdh** (possession) requirement
- Modern scholars extend this principle to paper currencies and digital money

---

### Problem 6 — Leverage (Trading With Borrowed Money)

#### What happens in Forex:

- Brokers offer leverage of **1:50, 1:100, up to 1:500**
- You are controlling massive positions with **mostly borrowed money**
- This borrowed capital has an **interest cost** (swap fee)
- You are taking enormous risk with money that **is not yours**

#### Why it is a problem in Islam:

- Borrowing money to **speculate** is not permitted in Islam
- Islamic finance allows borrowing for **genuine need and productive purpose**
- Borrowing to gamble on price movements serves **no productive economic purpose**
- It also creates the Riba problem since borrowed funds attract interest

---

### Problem 7 — Zero Sum Nature (La Darar — No Harm Principle)

#### What happens in Forex:

- Forex is a **zero sum game** — every pip one trader gains, another trader loses
- No real goods are produced, no services rendered, no value added to society
- Money simply moves from **losing traders to winning traders and brokers**

#### Why it is a problem in Islam:

- Islam strongly encourages economic activity that **benefits society**
- Trade should create **mutual benefit** for both parties
- The Prophet ﷺ said:

> **"There should be neither harming nor reciprocating harm"** _(Ibn Majah, Daraqutni)_

- Pure speculation that extracts wealth from others without creating value goes against the **spirit of Islamic economics**

---

### Problem 8 — Addiction and Destruction of Wealth

#### What happens in Forex:

- Forex trading is **highly addictive** — similar to gambling addiction
- The excitement of winning and the desperation to recover losses keeps traders hooked
- Studies show the vast majority of retail traders **lose all their deposited money**
- Many people have lost their **savings, homes, and families** to Forex addiction

#### Why it is a problem in Islam:

- Islam prohibits the **destruction of one's own wealth**:

> **"And do not throw yourselves into destruction"** _(Surah Al-Baqarah 2:195)_

- Islam also prohibits **consuming wealth unjustly**:

> **"O you who believe! Do not consume each other's wealth unjustly"** _(Surah Al-Nisa 4:29)_

---

### Problem 9 — Deceptive Marketing Targeting Muslims

#### What happens in Forex:

- Brokers specifically market **Islamic accounts** to Muslim traders
- They use terms like "Shariah compliant" and "halal trading" without genuine certification
- Swap fees are simply **renamed as administrative fees**
- Muslims are led to believe they are trading permissibly when the core problems remain

#### Why it is a problem in Islam:

- This is **Hiyal** — using legal tricks to make haram appear halal
- The Prophet ﷺ warned:

> **"The one who deceives us is not one of us"** _(Sahih Muslim)_

- Rebranding haram elements does not change their **fundamental nature**
