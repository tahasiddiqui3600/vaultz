[[FOREX AND CFDs]]
#### The Hadith on Currency Exchange (Most Direct Evidence)

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:

> **"Gold for gold, silver for silver, wheat for wheat, barley for barley, dates for dates, salt for salt — like for like, hand to hand. Whoever pays more or takes more has engaged in Riba. The giver and taker are equal in sin."** _(Sahih Muslim — narrated by Ubadah ibn al-Samit رضي الله عنه)_

This hadith establishes the **Bay' al-Sarf** principle — currency exchange must be:

- ✅ Equal in value (or market rate)
- ✅ **Hand to hand** (immediate, on the spot)
- ✅ No delay in settlement

Standard Forex trading often violates the **"hand to hand"** condition because positions are held open for days or weeks.

---

#### What Do Scholars Say?

---

##### 🟢 Scholars Who Say Forex CAN Be Halal (with strict conditions)

Notable among them is the **Accounting and Auditing Organization for Islamic Financial Institutions (AAOIFI)** and some contemporary scholars who argue:

- Currency exchange is a **genuine economic need**
- If conducted **spot (immediately)**, it satisfies Bay' al-Sarf conditions
- If **no swap/interest** is charged, Riba is avoided
- If used for **hedging real business risk**, not pure speculation, it may be permissible

**Conditions for permissibility:**

- ✅ Must be a **spot transaction** (T+2 settlement maximum)
- ✅ **No overnight swap/interest** charges
- ✅ **No excessive leverage** that leads to pure gambling
- ✅ Must have a **genuine economic purpose**, not pure speculation
- ✅ Full **ownership and delivery** must be intended

---

##### 🔴 Scholars Who Say Forex is Haram

The majority of traditional scholars including many at **Al-Azhar University** and the **Islamic Fiqh Academy** argue:

- Retail Forex is **dominated by speculation**, not real currency need
- Leverage makes it **indistinguishable from gambling**
- Swap-free accounts are a **legal trick (Hiyal)** that does not remove the underlying harm
- The entire structure of margin trading contradicts **Islamic principles of ownership**
- Brokers are often **Market Makers** — meaning they profit when you lose, creating a fundamentally exploitative relationship

---

#### Islamic Account (Swap-Free) — Does It Solve the Problem?

|Feature|Standard Account|Islamic Account|
|---|---|---|
|Overnight Swap|❌ Charged (Riba)|✅ Removed|
|Leverage|❌ Still present|❌ Still present|
|Speculation|❌ Still present|❌ Still present|
|Gharar|❌ Still present|❌ Still present|
|Fully Halal?|❌ No|⚠️ Debated|

> Most scholars agree that simply **removing the swap fee** does not automatically make Forex halal — the deeper issues of gharar, maysir, and leverage remain unresolved.

---

#### Summary — The Islamic Verdict

|Type of Forex Activity|Islamic Ruling|
|---|---|
|Spot currency exchange for travel/business|✅ Halal|
|Currency exchange for import/export hedging|✅ Halal (with conditions)|
|Retail Forex with swap fees|❌ Haram (Riba)|
|Highly leveraged speculation|❌ Haram (Maysir + Gharar)|
|Swap-free account with low leverage, real purpose|⚠️ Scholars differ|
|Pure day trading for profit with no real need|❌ Haram (majority view)|

---

#### 🔑 Final Takeaway

The **majority of Islamic scholars** consider conventional retail Forex trading — with its leverage, swaps, and pure speculation — to be **not permissible** due to the combination of Riba, Gharar, and Maysir. However, if currency exchange serves a **genuine real-world economic purpose**, is conducted on a **spot basis**, involves **no interest**, and uses **minimal or no leverage**, a minority of scholars consider it permissible under strict conditions.
