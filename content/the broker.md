### Who is a Broker in Forex Trading?

#### Simple Definition

A Forex broker is a **middleman company or institution** that gives you access to the foreign exchange market. Since the real Forex market (interbank market) is only accessible to large banks and financial institutions with millions of dollars, brokers act as a **gateway** for retail traders like you and me.Without a broker, a regular person **cannot access** Forex markets at all.
### The Three Pillars — Why Brokers Control Your Account

---

#### Pillar 1 — You Signed a Legal Agreement

- When you open an account, you sign a **terms & conditions document**
- Most people skip reading it, but it contains very important permissions
- By agreeing, you **legally give the broker** the right to close your trades, freeze your account, and change rules
- The broker does not steal this power — **you hand it over yourself** by clicking "I Agree"

---

#### Pillar 2 — The Broker is Lending You Money

- When you use leverage, **most of the money in your trade is the broker's money**, not yours
- Example: You deposit $100, broker lends you $9,900 — now 99% of the trade belongs to the broker
- Because it is **their money at risk**, they have every right to protect it
- If the trade goes bad, they will close it immediately — just like a **bank repossessing a car** when you stop paying loan installments

---

#### Pillar 3 — The Broker Physically Holds Your Money

- When you deposit funds, the money goes into **the broker's system**
- You only see a number on a screen — the **real cash sits with the broker**
- Since they are holding your money, they can deduct fees, absorb losses, and freeze your account whenever needed
- Think of it like a **wallet that belongs to you, but is locked inside someone else's safe**

the broker requires a fee on every trade.

The spread is the **difference between the Buy price (Ask) and the Sell price (Bid)** of a currency pair.
The **broker** is the primary beneficiary of the spread.
Every single trade you open — the broker earns the spread **instantly and automatically.**

Every time you click Buy or Sell, the **spread is automatically deducted** from your account and collected by your broker. It is how most brokers make their primary income — whether you win or lose your trade, **the broker always earns the spread.**
