### What are CFDs?

---

#### Simple Definition

CFD stands for **Contract for Difference.** It is an agreement between a trader and a broker to **exchange the difference in price** of an asset from when you open a trade to when you close it — without ever actually owning the underlying asset.

---

#### How it Works — Core Concept

```
You think Gold price will rise:

Gold price when you OPEN trade:  $1,900
Gold price when you CLOSE trade: $1,950
Difference:                      $50

You receive: $50 profit per unit ✅
(You never owned any actual Gold)
```

If the price moved against you:

```
Gold price when you OPEN trade:  $1,900
Gold price when you CLOSE trade: $1,850
Difference:                      $50

You pay: $50 loss per unit ❌
```

---

#### What Can You Trade as CFDs?

CFDs are available on almost any financial asset:

| Asset Class     | Examples                     |
| --------------- | ---------------------------- |
| **Currencies**  | EUR/USD, GBP/PKR, USD/JPY    |
| **Stocks**      | Apple, Tesla, Saudi Aramco   |
| **Commodities** | Gold, Oil, Silver, Wheat     |
| **Indices**     | S&P 500, Dow Jones, FTSE 100 |
| **Crypto**      | Bitcoin, Ethereum            |

#### 🔑 Key Takeaway

CFDs are essentially a **pure speculation instrument** designed specifically to trade price movements without any real asset changing hands. They combine **every major Islamic finance concern** — no ownership, interest charges, excessive uncertainty, leverage, and zero sum gambling — into one single product. This is why CFDs are considered **even more problematic than standard Forex trading** from an Islamic perspective and are rejected by virtually all Islamic finance scholars.
