### Margin Account in Forex Trading
[[FOREX AND CFDs]]

#### Basic Definition

A **margin account** is a type of trading account where your broker lends you money to control a position much larger than your actual deposited capital. Your deposited funds act as **collateral** (the "margin") against potential losses.

---

#### How It Works — Simple Example

|Your Deposit (Margin)|Leverage|Position You Can Control|
|---|---|---|
|$1,000|1:100|$100,000|
|$500|1:50|$25,000|

### Margin Balance in Forex Trading

#### What is Margin Balance?

**Margin Balance** (also called **Account Equity**) is the **real-time total value** of your trading account at any given moment. It reflects what your account is actually worth _right now_, including all open trades.
|Scenario |Calculation |Equity|
|---|---|---|
|You deposit $5,000, no trades open   |$5,000 + $0|     $5,000**|
|Open trade currently up $300     |$5,000 + $300|    **$5,300**|
|Open trade currently down $800|    $5,000 - $800|     $4,200**|

if a trader goes down on balance the broker sends a margin call and if the trader does not act upon the call after a certain amount of time the broker closes the account
